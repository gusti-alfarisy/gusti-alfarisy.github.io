## About

Hi..! I am **Gusti Ahmad Fanshuri Alfarisy, Ph.D.**, a lecturer and researcher in Computer Science with research interests at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, Environmental Informatics, and Agroecological Informatics**.

My research focuses on developing intelligent systems that can operate in dynamic and open-world environments. I am particularly interested in applying Artificial Intelligence and computational methods to real-world problems in **biodiversity, environmental monitoring, and agroecology**.

### Research Interests

My current research interests include:

- Open-World Learning and Open-Set Recognition
- Continual and Lifelong Machine Learning
- Biodiversity Monitoring
- Environmental and Agroecological Informatics
- Computer Vision
- Edge Artificial Intelligence
- Geospatial and Remote Sensing Analytics

### Research Direction

My long-term research direction is to develop intelligent systems capable of **learning, adapting, and acquiring new knowledge from changing environments**.

A major part of my current work explores how Artificial Intelligence can contribute to understanding and monitoring natural environments, particularly in **Kalimantan (Borneo)**. This includes plant species recognition, biodiversity knowledge systems, environmental monitoring, IoT-based sensing, and aerial image analytics.

### Environmental & Agroecological Informatics Research Group

I coordinate the **Environmental & Agroecological Informatics Research Group (EAIR)**, an emerging academic research group that brings together research and student projects at the intersection of computing, Artificial Intelligence, biodiversity, environmental science, and agroecology.

Current activities include biodiversity informatics, intelligent species exploration, environmental monitoring, smart soil sensing, and aerial image analytics.
