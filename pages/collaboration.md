# Research Collaboration

I am open to *research collaborations that align with and strengthen the research direction of the Environmental & Agroecological Informatics Research Group (EAIR Lab)*.

Our primary interest is in the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, Environmental Informatics, Agroecology, and Biodiversity**, particularly research that can contribute to scientific publications, datasets, intelligent systems, and long-term research initiatives.

---

## Research Areas

I am particularly interested in collaborations related to:

### Artificial Intelligence for Biodiversity

- Plant and species recognition
- Biodiversity informatics
- Open-set and open-world species recognition
- Continual and lifelong learning for biodiversity
- Intelligent biodiversity information systems
- AI-assisted biodiversity monitoring

### Environmental & Agroecological Informatics

- Environmental monitoring and analytics
- Agroecological information systems
- AI for sustainable agriculture
- Soil and environmental sensing
- IoT-based environmental monitoring
- Environmental data analytics
- Decision-support systems for agriculture and ecosystems

### Open-World & Lifelong Artificial Intelligence

I am particularly interested in developing AI systems capable of operating in dynamic real-world environments where new classes, species, objects, or environmental conditions may emerge over time.

Potential topics include:

- Open-set recognition
- Open-world recognition
- Novelty and unknown detection
- Continual learning
- Lifelong machine learning
- Novel class discovery
- Domain-specific open-world AI
